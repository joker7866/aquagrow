# AquaGrow – My Garden & Aquarium: Privacy Policy

_Effective: 8 October 2026_

AquaGrow is an offline-first app for looking after a home garden and freshwater aquariums. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

## The short version

- There is no account and no sign-in.
- There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- Everything you enter stays on your phone unless you choose to export it or use the optional assistant.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores what you enter in its private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.

- plants, tanks, fish and their details
- care logs: watering, fertilizer, growth, harvests, feeding, water tests, maintenance
- tasks and reminder settings
- inventory items and expenses
- photos you take or choose
- app settings: modules, theme, currency, display name

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

## Backups and calendar export

When you create a backup or export your calendar, the app writes a file to a location **you** pick in the system file picker (for example, your phone's storage or a cloud drive app). What happens to that file afterwards is up to you and the storage service you chose. The app only reads a backup file when you pick one to restore.

## Optional AI assistant

The assistant is **off by default**. When you turn it on in Profile, you are shown what it sends before it is enabled.

When you ask a question, the app sends the following to the assistant server configured in the app:

- your question and the earlier messages in the current chat
- only if you choose to attach them: the selected plant's or tank's details, its recent logs, and a photo

The assistant server passes this to an AI model provider (Anthropic) to generate an answer and does not store it. Chats are not saved in the app; leaving the chat screen clears them. Nothing is sent until you ask a question, and nothing is sent while the assistant is off.

Answers are general suggestions, not diagnoses. The assistant cannot diagnose fish or plant illness.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Camera | Only when you choose to take a plant or fish photo. |
| Notifications | To show the care reminders you set up. |
| Run at startup | To restore your reminders after the phone restarts or the time zone changes. |
| Internet / network state | Only used by the optional assistant. |

## Children

AquaGrow is a general-audience hobby app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings. This deletes all app data from the phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere are not affected; delete them yourself if you want.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the app update and the effective date above will change.
